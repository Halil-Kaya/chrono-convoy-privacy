Common questions

I bought No Ads or the Starter Pack on another device. Open the shop (the + next to your gems) and tap RESTORE.

A purchase did not arrive. Close and reopen the game, then tap RESTORE in the shop. If it is still missing, email us with the purchase date.

How do I ask for a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change my ad choices? In the game, open Settings → Ad Choices (where it applies).
