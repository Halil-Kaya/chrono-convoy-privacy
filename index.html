Chrono Convoy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026 (gameplay analytics and crash reports added)

Chrono Convoy ("the game") is made by Halil Kaya ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat, and we do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (coins, gems, upgrades, the zone you reached, best distance) and your settings (sound, music, volume, vibration) are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos, and an occasional full-screen ad between runs. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the device model and operating system, an approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, and, only if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad Choices. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Buying No Ads or the Starter Pack removes the full-screen ads between runs.

Gameplay analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To see where players get stuck and to fix bugs, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Firebase Analytics: for example when a run starts and ends, how far it got and what ended it, which zone was cleared, which upgrade or item was bought, and whether an ad was watched. Firebase also records an app-instance identifier, the device model, operating system, app version and an approximate country. If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report with the device state and the last gameplay events. This data is not used to identify you. See Firebase's privacy information.

In-app purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Purchases are made and paid for through Apple's App Store; we never see your payment details. The game uses RevenueCat to check which purchases belong to you so that No Ads can be restored on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and basic device information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game is not directed at children.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent in the game under Settings → Ad Choices (where it applies).
  • On iPhone, limit ad tracking under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete the game to remove all data stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
